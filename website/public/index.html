Install in three steps

  1. Download and unzip AwakeBar.
  2. Move the app to Applications.
  3. Open it and choose a duration from the cup.

The current build is ad hoc signed and not notarized. macOS may require explicit approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security. You can also build from source.

What about automatic locking?

AwakeBar prevents idle display sleep and periodically declares user activity. Manual locking, lid closure, forced sleep, and managed policies can still apply. Automatic-lock behavior depends on your macOS settings. AwakeBar does not unlock a locked Mac.
